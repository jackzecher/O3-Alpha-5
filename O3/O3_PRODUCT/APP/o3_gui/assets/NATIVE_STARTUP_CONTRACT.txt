O3 Alpha 4 native startup and branding

Alpha 4 restores the original O3.exe, bootstrap, runtime profile and supervised
workspace entry points byte-for-byte from the protected Alpha 2 / Candidate 6
payload. The newer application uses its current icon and appearance. The native
executable retains its original resources and remains the sole startup splash
and launch-failure diagnostic owner. No competing Qt splash is started by the
native entry point.

The welcome-screen design and Appearance preview remain available in the GUI.
Their preference does not control the protected native launcher's splash.
Executable icon/splash restyling requires the original launcher build source
and separate Windows validation; it is outside this startup restoration.

Editable icon masters and derived PNG/ICO assets in this folder are retained
unchanged. AI_RUNTIME runs the actual application in a disposable synthetic
Linux workspace; it does not replace Windows startup or qualify NIPR operation.
